What the video shows

The recording opens on Night Court, then shows highlights from a genuine two-minute Game Center match between an iPhone and an iPad. Both players see each shot. A basket keeps possession; a miss passes the turn. Each possession lasts 15 seconds, with two points for a basket and three for a clean swish.

The badges tour shows the actual collection and unlock requirements: First Spark for 10 consecutive baskets, Heatwave for 50, and Night Legend for 100 in solo Arcade or Practice; Court Crown, Silver Flight, and Bronze Rise for confirmed global ranks 1, 2, and 3. Online scores do not count toward these offline streak records, leaderboards, or badges. The video ends with offline practice.

Footage comes from the current app running on two physical devices. The highlight edit condenses a complete match and does not fabricate scores, badge unlocks, or game screens. English captions identify each section.
