Privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Night Court is developed by Mahmut Aoata. This policy explains how the iPhone and iPad game and its support pages handle information.

Data collection in the game

Night Court does not operate a gameplay backend or collect gameplay data for the developer. Gameplay works offline without a Game Center account. The game has no advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

Optional Game Center participation

If you sign in to Apple’s Game Center, Night Court automatically publishes your longest completed basket streak to Apple-hosted daily, weekly, and repeating 30-day leaderboards. Your Game Center display name, score, and rank can appear publicly or to Game Center friends. Achievement progress is sent to Apple to restore your earned badges across devices. Apple processes this information under Game Center & Privacy; the developer does not receive a copy from a developer-operated service.

You can stop future score publishing in Night Court Settings or the leaderboard sheet. This setting applies to the signed-in player on this installation; it does not remove scores already published. Achievement restoration and synchronization remain available. Sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings to keep all new play local. Guest badges never transfer to a signed-in player.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your personal best score, best streak, selected ball skin, sound and haptic preferences, and tutorial completion in local app storage. Night Court also stores completed streaks, pending best scores, badge progress, and the publishing preference separately for each Game Center player and for guests. These records support offline play and safe retries within their original leaderboard periods. They are not sent to the developer.

iOS may include app data in a device backup according to your Apple settings. Night Court does not operate its own cloud synchronization. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore that data.

Support email

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address and information you include. We use those messages to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please send only information needed for support. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us at the address below.

This website and external links

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when serving the website under GitHub’s privacy statement. Links opened from the game or website are subject to the destination’s policies.

Children’s privacy

Offline gameplay is available to players of all ages. Game Center availability and its social features depend on Apple account, parental-control, and system settings. Night Court does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the game. A parent or guardian may contact us about information voluntarily sent to support.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
