Privacy policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Night Court is developed by Mahmut Aoata. This policy explains how the iPhone and iPad game and its support pages handle information.

Data collection in the game

Night Court does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data from gameplay. The game works offline and has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking, or developer-operated servers. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your personal best score, best streak, selected ball skin, sound and haptic preferences, and tutorial completion in local app storage. This information is used only to remember your choices and records. It is not sent to the developer.

iOS may include app data in a device backup according to your Apple settings. Night Court does not operate its own cloud synchronization. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore that data.

Support email

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address and information you include. We use those messages to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please send only information needed for support. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us at the address below.

This website and external links

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when serving the website under GitHub’s privacy statement. Links opened from the game or website are subject to the destination’s policies.

Children’s privacy

The same no-collection gameplay design applies to players of all ages. Night Court does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the game. A parent or guardian may contact us about information voluntarily sent to support.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support-data requests, contact Mahmut Aoata at mahmutaoata@gmail.com.
